Technical remarks
1. Outer tank diameter: Ø560 mm.
2. Inner tank diameter: Ø460 mm.
3. Insulation: polyurethane foam, 50 mm.
4. Tolerance of cylinder height ±10 mm, tolerance of nozzle heights ±3 mm.
5. Tank surface smooth and clean, without dents or bulges.
6. Inner tank pressure test 0.8–1.0 MPa for 3 minutes: no leakage, no pressure
drop, no deformation.
7. White PG7 cable glands on temperature sensor pockets.
8. Packaging: top, bottom and 3 intermediate styrofoam protectors, carton box with
REO HEATING logo.
