Features

Designed for
commercial applications

RH X50 combines maximum efficiency with versatility. It covers heating, cooling and hot water, and cascade control lets it grow with the building's demand.

  • R290 refrigerant (propane) — ODP = 0, low GWP = 3.
  • Dual DC inverter technology: precise and efficient heating with a water outlet temperature of up to 78 °C.
  • Top efficiency rating: ErP A+++ (at 35 °C), COP up to 5.62.
  • Built-in Wi‑Fi module for connectivity and remote control.
  • Multilingual interface — easy operation in many languages.
  • SG Ready (Smart Grid): better energy management and smart grid integration.
  • Automatic temperature control for optimal performance.
  • Night mode with reduced noise during off-hours.
  • Cascade control of up to 8 units for flexible scaling.
  • Timed high-temperature disinfection for safe and hygienic hot water.
  • IoT cloud platform for remote monitoring and management.
  • Options: built-in circulation pumps, refrigerant leak sensor, control of an external heat source and hot water tank.
