Test History
These appliances are Air To Water Heat Pump Unit, each one including a whole compression
type refrigerant circuit to heat water in another circuit. These appliances were for cooling and
heating water function, this report only for heating capacity test.
The main power for models PW06-DKXLRE1-H8, PW08-DKXLRE1-H8, PW10-DKXLRE1-H8 is
supplied by a 3-pole supply cord connecting to fixed wiring.
The main power for models PW08-DKXLRE3-H8, PW10-DKXLRE3-H8, PW12-DKXLRE3-H8,
PW14-DKXLRE3-H8, PW16-DKXLRE3-H8 is supplied by a 5-pole supply cord connecting to
fixed wiring.
Water enthalpy method was adopted in this report.
Standby mode power, off mode power and thermostat-off mode power were tested according to
clause 12 of standard EN 14825:2022.
According to the manufacturer's requirements, the test voltage is 230V~, 50Hz for models
PW06-DKXLRE1-H8, PW08-DKXLRE1-H8, PW10-DKXLRE1-H8.
According to the manufacturer's requirements, the test voltage is 400V~, 50Hz for models
PW08-DKXLRE3-H8, PW10-DKXLRE3-H8, PW12-DKXLRE3-H8, PW14-DKXLRE3-H8, PW16-
