--- snímek 10 ---
Antifreeze Drain Protection(Optional)
The heat pump is standard with an 
antifreeze drain valve
, it can automatically judge the operation state of the system. When the system is shut down or power off unexpectedly, the antifreeze mode can be opened in time:when the valve sense the temp drops to 
3
°
C
, 
the valve will drain water to prevent system freezing.
With the decrease of water temperature, the antifreeze valve can automatically increase the drainage speed, effectively balance the heat loss in the system. The valve will be automatically closed when the water temperature rises to 4 
°
C.
In the event of a malfunction or maintainance, the Freeze Protection Valve (1) can be replaced. 
An automatic shut-off cock (2) prevents the water from draining while the cartridge is being replaced, thereby keeping the system pressurised.
When maintaining the vacumm breaker(3), remove the breaker cap, clean the internal float and sealing pad, and then reinstall them in order.

--- snímek 12 ---
§
14a EnWG-Make HP More Energy-efficient
“
§
14a EnWG” is Germany’s regulation for controllable energy devices — helping the grid stay stable, and helping you save money.
Customers can connect the unit to the energy management system via 
RS485 Modbus
. During peak electricity consumption periods, the unit's power can be remotely temporarily reduced or suspended to help stabilize the power grid operation.
Users can choose whether to enable the power limit function on the Modbus control system and set the power limit value to control the reduction of compressor power during peak power consumption, 
ensuring that the unit reduces power to 4.2 kW 
without shutting down and can still operate at a lower power. 
On the one hand, it ensures a certain capacity supply indoors and ensures comfort; On the other hand, it can reduce energy consumption and maintain the stability of the power grid.
