We are preparing noise assessments for several installations in the Czech Republic, including cascades of 2–4 units at apartment buildings. Local authorities assess the night-time limit (40 dB(A) at the neighbour's façade, or 35 dB(A) if the noise has tonal components). For this we need more acoustic data than we currently have.

At the moment we have the TÜV SÜD reports 64.181.23.00497.01, 64.181.23.00422.02 and 64.181.23.00458.01 (EN 12102-1:2022). These cover only the rating points A7/W35 and A7/W55. Could you please send us the following for PW030-DKZLRS-E/S, PW040-DKZLRS-E/S, PW050-DKZLRS-E and -E/S, and PW060-DKZLRS-E:

1. **KEYMARK data sheets** from the Heat Pump KEYMARK database, including the sound power level. Please also tell us whether KEYMARK certificates exist for the R290 -E versions of PW030, PW040 and PW050. We only have certificate 041-K032-10 for PW060-DKZLRS-E.
2. **Sound power level LWA (EN 12102-1) at maximum compressor frequency and maximum fan speed.**
3. **LWA at low outdoor temperatures:** A2, A−7, A−10 and, if available, A−15 °C, each at W35 and W55. Alternatively, a table of LWA against compressor frequency and fan speed.
4. **LWA in Silent mode / Silent time and in Powerful mode**, together with the maximum compressor frequency, fan speed and heating capacity in each mode.
5. **Octave-band spectrum** of LWA (63 Hz – 8 kHz), ideally 1/3-octave, for the operating points above.
6. **Tonal components:** a statement or measurement for the compressor, fan and inverter (e.g. according to ISO 1996-2), or at least the 1/3-octave spectrum.
7. **Maximum compressor frequency and maximum fan speed** of each model. In the TÜV reports the units ran at 48–58 Hz and 400 rpm. Please tell us what percentage of maximum capacity this represents.
8. **Clarification for PW040-DKZLRS-E/S:** your EU 811/2013 data sheet states LWA 60 dB(A) at 35 °C, but TÜV report 64.181.23.00422.02 (p. 11) states 57 dB(A). Please confirm which value is correct. The TÜV reports also list the declared sound power level as "N/A". Please send the declared values and their tolerance.
9. **Definition of the catalogue values** "Noise ≤ 47 / 50 / 52 / 53 dB(A)" in the price list and user manual: is this sound power or sound pressure, at what distance, at which operating point, and according to which standard?
10. **Written confirmation** that the units supplied to us as RH030-PW8-A2, RH040-PW12-A2, RH050-PW15-A3 and RH060-PW22-A3 are identical in construction to the tested models PW030-DKZLRS-E/S, PW040-DKZLRS-E/S, PW050-DKZLRS-E and PW060-DKZLRS-E.
11. Also, if available: noise during defrost and start-up (LAmax), directivity (air outlet compared with the inlet and sides), measurement uncertainty, and recommended anti-vibration mounts.

Original laboratory reports are best, even if only for some of the points. If some data do not exist, please tell us, so that we can plan our own measurements.
