--- snímek 16 ---
§
14a EnWG-Make HP More Energy-efficient
“
§
14a EnWG” is Germany’s regulation for controllable energy devices — helping the grid stay stable, and helping you save money.
Customers can connect the unit to the energy management system via 
RS485 Modbus
. During peak electricity consumption periods, the unit's power can be remotely temporarily reduced or suspended to help stabilize the power grid operation.
Users can choose whether to enable the power limit function on the Modbus control system and set the power limit value to control the reduction of compressor power during peak power consumption, 
ensuring that the unit reduces power to 4.2 kW 
without shutting down and can still operate at a lower power. 
On the one hand, it ensures a certain capacity supply indoors and ensures comfort; On the other hand, it can reduce energy consumption and maintain the stability of the power grid.

SG-Ready & PV-Ready
This Heat Pump supports connect smart grid and photovoltaic system to store energy. The heat pump determines the power status through the SG and EVU signal status, and adjusts the operating mode according to different power grid statuses.
When connecting the SG and EVU signal, the heat pump will run in hot water mode priority to heat the water, then, according to peak electricity consumption or other conditions, provide hot water to users. 
It can store energy well, monitor energy use and allocate it reasonably, and reduce energy consumption.

--- snímek 28 ---
Timer Setting
Timer ON/OFF: 
On the timer interface, click on "Timer On/Off" to enter the timer on/off settings interface;
Sterilization Timer: 
On the timer interface, click on "Sterilization Timer" to enter the timer settings interface;
Manually start sterilization:
 If the current status is 'off ', clicking the 'OK' button will manually activate the sterilization function (the set timed sterilization is still effective).
Silent timer: 
On the timer interface, click on "Silent Timer" to enter the timer on/off settings interface;
There are two levels in silent mode, 
level 1 and level 2
. The maximum speed of fan and compressor in level 2 mode is smaller than that in level 1.

Holiday Function
Enter the holiday  mode setting interface, set the start and end time, heating mode, hot water mode, and whether automatic sterilization is enabled. After setting, set "Function Use" to "use" to enable it, and set it to "unused" to cancel or disable the function;
User parameter 18 HOLIDAY_AWAY_ThS sets the heat pump’s water set temp for space heating mode when in holiday away mode.
User parameter 
19 HOLIDAY_AWAY_ThwS sets the heat pump’s water set temp for DHW mode when in holiday away mode.
Remark: Holiday Home has the same setting as above.

--- snímek 35 ---
Advanced Settings
For installation, service, and other needs, authorized engineers or service personnel can modify the parameters of the controller through the following path and enter a password.
Main interface→Right swipe page→Advanced setting, enter the correct password in the following interface and click the "OK" button to enter the advanced settings.
In the parameter configuration, we can set electric heating control parameters, SG function, underfloor heating, solar water pump, terminal water pump, etc.
